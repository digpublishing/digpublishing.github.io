Tom Bernard of the Cleveland Museum of Art and Valerie Hellstein of the Denver Art Museum present two case studies for how to develop a robust strategy for leadership and colleagues. Exploring the “why” of digital publishing, this session addresses the financial, scheduling, and staffing considerations to help you make the case for a digital, hybrid, or print-only production.
