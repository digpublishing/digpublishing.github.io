We often talk about difficulties around the discovery of born-digital books. Hear from two major distributors of digital publications. Benjamin Tiven, co-director of Library Stack (https://www.librarystack.org/), and Phillip Hearn, senior publisher relations manager at Project MUSE (https://muse.jhu.edu/), discuss the role of open access, how they use metadata for enhanced discovery, how they’re exploring the use of AI, and more.
